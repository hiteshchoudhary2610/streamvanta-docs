Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

StreamVanta Studio ("we", "us", or "our") operates the StreamVanta Studio mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our App.

1. Information We Collect

When you use StreamVanta Studio, we may collect:

  • Account information: Your Google email address, display name, and profile picture (via Google Sign-In).
  • YouTube channel metadata: Channel ID, title, thumbnail, and subscriber count (only when you explicitly connect your YouTube account).
  • Streaming settings: Your chosen RTMP server URL and stream key, stored securely on your device only.
  • Payment information: UPI transaction reference (UTR) and plan chosen. We do NOT collect or store card, bank, or UPI credentials.
  • Device information: Device model and Android version, used to manage device limits on your account.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and provide access to your account.
  • To enable screen streaming to YouTube via your connected channel.
  • To manage your premium subscription and payment records.
  • To provide customer support.

3. What We Do NOT Collect

  • We do NOT record, store, or transmit your live video streams.
  • We do NOT collect your contacts, SMS, call logs, or location.
  • We do NOT store your YouTube access tokens on our servers.
  • We do NOT sell your data to third parties.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your account data is stored securely on Supabase (a PostgreSQL-based cloud database) with industry-standard encryption. Stream keys and sensitive settings are stored locally on your device using Android Keystore encryption.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google Sign-In: For authentication. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • YouTube Data API: For reading your channel metadata. You may revoke access anytime from your Google Account permissions.
  • Supabase: For secure data storage. Governed by Supabase's Privacy Policy.

6. Your Rights

You can:

  • Access and update your profile at any time from the Account screen.
  • Disconnect your YouTube channel from the App.
  • Delete your account by contacting us at support@streamvanta.app.
  • Revoke Google permissions from your Google account settings.

7. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@streamvanta.app.
